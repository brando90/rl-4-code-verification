# QA of Plan 1 (second model, max effort), 10-28-2026 — verdict: PASS WITH FIXES

**TLDR:** An independent reviewer (a different and stronger model than the author, max effort) checked the plan Google Doc and this repository. Verdict PASS WITH FIXES: the design is the right smallest comparison and the numbers are mostly true; 13 defects were found and all are accepted. Each fix is applied in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) (draft, not locked).

## Defects and fixes (severity)
1. (high) The "mismatch above 1%" stop rule is unenforceable as coded: `train_e20.py` computes the gate only with `--smoke` and reports the maximum at the end. Fix: every pilot and main update runs with the mismatch computed, and aborts above 1% or on any non-finite value.
2. (high) The gate was to be measured on the wrong device and path: bf16 already measured 6.42% on A4000 (Exp 21), so "bf16 plus IS if it passes" is dead on arrival (IS reweights; Exp 22 finding 4). Fix: say bf16 is expected to fail; measure the gate in the trainer loop on the GPU that trains, at the chosen lr.
3. (high) The lr pilot was on arm A only, "reward does not move" was undefined, and arm asymmetry was unaddressed: with group-normalised advantages B back-propagates about 13 of 16 groups against about 2 of 16 for A, so B's per-update step is about 6x larger at equal lr. Fix: pilot A and B at both lrs (4 x 12 updates); pre-registered rule (largest lr at which both arms pass gate and finiteness and neither strict training reward falls; tie goes to 1e-6); numeric definition of "moves" (mean strict successes per 128 over updates 7 to 12 minus updates 1 to 6 at least +2 points); log trained_sequences, grad_norm and IS min/max per arm.
4. (med) Start policy mislabeled. Exp 20 used the Sherlock v4 seed 20260831 replicate (5.37%); Exps 21 to 24 used v1 step_658 (SNAP, 4.91% = 53/1080). Phase 0's 4.43% and 13.7% were measured on v4. Fix: name v1 step_658 with its weights SHA; label the Phase 0 numbers as v4.
5. (med) The +1.5-point threshold and the power were not justified: noise SE about 0.6 point per arm-seed, B minus A SE about 0.6 to 0.8, power about 50% at a true 1.5-point effect, minimum detectable effect about 2 points. Fix: state the MDE; evaluate update 48 at 135 x 32 samples or add a third seed; keep 8 samples at updates 16 and 32; secondaries are descriptive.
6. (med) Cost understated: 13 minutes per update was with the stand-in reward; real scoring adds 3 to 5 minutes, so 4 x 48 updates is 51 to 58 A100-hours, plus pilot 6 to 13 and smoke 1 to 2: about 60 to 75 A100-hours, 30 to 38 hours wall on two A100s; 1.6 GPU-hours per evaluation is an L40S number. Fix: per-phase GPU-hour caps with stop-at-cap.
7. (med) Hardware plan has unlisted engineering: Lean scoring is synchronous on the training node; "scoring on mercury" needs a remote path that does not exist; evaluations on mercury need Lean plus Mathlib, scorer pin 59059a20, a non-Slurm driver and checkpoint rsync; the fp16 trainer exists only as Exp 24 `train_e24.py` with `--reward-proxy`. The A4000 blockers are the 6.9 GB fp32 gradient buffer and vLLM at 0.75 memory (Adam and master weights are on CPU). Fix: list the code to land and test before stage 1 (fp16 flags with real reward in one trainer, local scorer, local evaluation driver, CPU gradient accumulation, vLLM about 0.5 on A4000); add disk: about 20 GB per run for optimizer state.
8. (med) Stage 0 agreement check needs Phase 0 Lean sources that are not in the repo and sit on Sherlock (Duo). Fix: use SNAP-resident scored files (Exp 13 T2 attempt_3 cells, or Exp 08 validation raw.jsonl plus per_task.csv).
9. (med) Missing: update-0 validation of step_658 through the new pipeline, cross-checked to 53/1080, before any trained checkpoint is scored; and a Goodhart audit (smooth up while strict, compile or theorem count down) plus adapter-strict logged in arm B.
10. (med) No freeze list. Fix: see PROTOCOL section "Freeze list".
11. (low) TIS wording: the Exp 20 recipe has it always on, and Exp 23 and 24 fp16 lanes ran with it (ratios 0.58 to 1.20). Keep it on in both arms and dtypes.
12. (low) Literature wording: arXiv 2602.01826 never mentions fp16 or bf16; MiniMax-M1 and ScaleRL are fp32 lm_head fixes (which made it worse in Exp 21 and 22); "fp16 gave 0.06%" is 0.06 to 0.08%; Exp 13 v3 "flat reward" was Qwen3-4B on NeMo RL; Varun settings differ (weight decay 0 vs 0.01; 128 vs 512 rollouts per update).
13. (low) "Public" means the Harbor registry release `veribench@1.3`; the GitHub repo `brando90/veribench` returns 404 anonymously (private). Name the registry and both pins.
