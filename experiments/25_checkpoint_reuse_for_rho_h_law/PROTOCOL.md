## 0b R2 prefix extrapolation (toy) and retrospective Night 4 to Night 5 check
C0 rule (all fits in 0b): grid over log C0 in [log 10, log 4000], 41 points, minimizing training RMSE, passed as `fit_utility_model(..., C0=c)`; the fixed training-median default is a reported sensitivity. Budgets are in tokens (512 per step): the grid is C0 = 512 × step_grid with step_grid log-spaced on [10, 4000] steps (41 points), i.e. C0 in [5,120, 2,048,000] tokens.
Fits, each of `fit_utility_model(RHO_H)` on observations (scale n1 to n3, all 16 environments):
- FULL: steps 25, 50, 100. PREFIX50: steps 25, 50. PREFIX25: step 25 only (C0 not identifiable from one budget: the fixed-median default is used and the cell is labeled so; the model is rank-deficient and `lstsq` returns the minimum-norm solution).
- Held-out targets: (i) PRIMARY: step 100 at the held-out scale n4 (fits use only n1 to n3); (ii) SECONDARY: step 100 at n3 with fits on n1 to n2 (labeled n3-holdout); (iii) in-scale: step 100 at n1 to n3 from the PREFIX fit (extrapolation in budget only).
- Scores: RMSE over the 16 environments at (scale, step 100) in pp; pairwise rank accuracy at (scale, step 100) (`pairwise_rank_accuracy`); each reported by scale and separately for cells with observed U < 0 at step 100 (n4 forgetting regime); uncertainty is the range over the 10 distinct multisets of the seed indices {0,1,2} (resample seeds with replacement, 3 draws), reported as min and max beside the 3-seed point estimate (3 seeds give no finer interval). Everything is reported with n_cells, n_trajectories.
- Sigmoid per-cell (ScaleRL analogue) arm: needs at least 3 points per cell trajectory and a prefix has 2, so it is declared NOT FITTABLE on this data and deferred to Night 6 (stated, not silently dropped).
- Retrospective cross-start check: fit FULL on Night 4 (n1 to n3), predict Night 5 at all four scales and steps 25, 50, 100 using Night 5's probes (Night 5 start checkpoints are new; environments, target prompts, pools and RL seeds are shared). Baselines: persistence (predict Night 5's U by Night 4's U at the same scale, environment and step) and the cross-environment mean of Night 4 at that scale and step. Labeled RETROSPECTIVE (outcomes are committed); never called a forecast.

## 0c R4 on real data
For each of the 20 SFT ladder rungs, the 4 Base models and the 8 fresh 1.7B runs (v27 12.5%, 25%, 50% × seeds 20260830 / 20260831 and v16 100% × the same two seed ids): ρ, H, q_mixed, admission rate, mean tokens and mean reward with a task-level bootstrap interval (resample the 135 tasks with all 8 samples, 10,000 draws, seed 20261028; draws with ρ ≤ 0 under either arm are dropped from the log ρ interval and counted).
P4 comparison: for f in {50%, 100%}: Δ_f = metric(1.7B ladder rung f) − mean over the 2 fresh seeds of metric(fresh f seed); pooled Δ = mean of Δ_50 and Δ_100; paired bootstrap (the same resampled task indices for the rung and both fresh seeds). The 12.5% and 25% rungs are declared uninformative before P4 is read (at most 3 mixed groups per cell, H about 1/135, ρ about 0). Fresh-seed difference |seed A − seed B| is reported as the training-noise yardstick; training-seed variability is otherwise not in the bootstrap (caveat).

## Pre-registered predictions (frozen)
Tolerances are in percentage points of utility. Intervals always carry n_cells, n_trajectories, n_prompts.
- **P2 (R2, retrospective on Night 4/5 data; the Night 6 version is a separate frozen test).** PRIMARY: RMSE at (n4, step 100) of the PREFIX50 fit <= 1.25 × the FULL fit's RMSE on the same cells, AND rank accuracy of the PREFIX50 fit at (n4, step 100) within 0.10 (10 points) of the FULL fit's own. SECONDARY: the same at (n3, step 100) with fits on n1 to n2. Both are reported; the verdict word is HOLDS only if the primary holds, WEAK if only the secondary holds, FAILS otherwise. The 70% rank threshold is a 02B criterion and is not used here (the toy law's rank accuracy was near chance at n4: Spearman −0.10, −0.21). The saturating-form arm is the only arm scored; the sigmoid arm is not fittable (see 0b). By scale and separately for cells with U < 0 at step 100.
- **P4 (R4, retrospective: data committed).** Pooled 50% and 100% rungs, paired task bootstrap, 10,000 draws. H: equivalence margin ±0.073 (0.25 × 0.290 standardizer), demonstrable with the data. log ρ: margin ±0.49 (0.5 × 0.976); the 0.25-SD margin (±0.244) is below what these data can show (half-width about 0.33). Intervals are reported against both margins; inside ±0.49 but not ±0.244 is labeled "equivalent at the coarse margin only". The verdict word per metric: EQUIVALENT (interval inside the margin), NOT EQUIVALENT (interval entirely outside), or INCONCLUSIVE (the rest).
- **P6 (Q1, exploratory but pre-set).** At each of 3.4B and 9.7B on >= 4 measurable math environments: the seed SD of log ρ and of H across the 3 Delphi replicas <= 0.25 × the between-environment SD at that scale, and the environment ranking by ρ and by H agrees across replicas (Spearman >= 0.8 per pair). With 3 replicas there are 2 degrees of freedom; ranges are reported. If it fails, 02B needs a second warm start at >= 1 scale.
- **P7 (Q2, exploratory).** Within each ladder (N at compute-optimal D; D at fixed N; the `laion` SFT ladder), over measurable cells only: |Spearman| of log ρ and of H against the ladder axis >= 0.8, and the fit through the smaller rungs predicts the largest (log ρ within 0.25 nat; H within 0.073). Cells below the bar are listed, never fitted.
- **P8 (Q3, exploratory).** Per curve with >= 40 steps (Snowball sync, async, 5.7T; Delphi D1 to D4 if public): a sigmoid fitted on the unsmoothed blend-average outcome reward from 10% to 50% of steps predicts 50 to 100% with MAE <= 0.10 of the curve's observed range; the Eq. 7 form fitted the same way is reported beside. With 7 or fewer curves nothing is "tested"; it is a worked example, per-domain curves normalized by each domain's upper bound. Q3 stops if no per-step reward data are public.
- **P9 (Q4, exploratory, one scale).** Rank the four sets by `env_term(ρ, H)` of a `UtilityModel` with the paper's β = (−0.006221, −0.008124, 0.023489, −0.076673, 0.093314) and the standardizer means and SDs replaced by the four sets' own; report the rankings by ρ alone and by H alone beside it. Pass if the top set has the largest published MATH-500 gain; n = 4, reported either way.
- Reference only (Night 6, not run here): P1 (dense curve RMSE_A <= RMSE_B + one seed SD), P3 (rolling form beats start-only and online baselines), P5 (schedule control).

## Outcome rules
One outcome label per plan §5 (ADOPT, ADOPT FOR DENSE CHECKPOINTS ONLY, NO ADVANTAGE, WRONG DOMAIN) needs Stage 1 and the real pilot, which are not part of this task; this task reports P2, P4 and P6 to P9 as method evidence only. Never rerun, drop or replace a cell because of its outcome; report as is. Deviations from this protocol are logged in `results.md` under "Deviations", with the reason, and are never silent.
