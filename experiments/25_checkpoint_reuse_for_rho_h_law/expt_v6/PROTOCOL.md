# Night 6 (expt_v6) PROTOCOL: dense versus independent checkpoint reuse on the GINC toy, with a schedule control

TLDR: One run read at many checkpoints (A) versus separate fresh runs of different lengths (B) at equal generated tokens, plus a cosine-schedule control. Frozen before any Night 6 outcome is read. Source plan: Doc "rl4cv: should the rho-H predictive law reuse checkpoints" v5, Stage 1 and §5 (P1, P2, P3, P5); the same text is committed by AT-1789 in the experiment folder. P4 and P6 to P9 belong to Stage 0 (AT-1789), not to this file.

Night 5 `experiments/16_toy_env_generation_ginc_hmm_rho_h_law/expt_v5/results/night5/`: `start_n{1..4}.pt` (hash-checked by `load_model`), `n*_meta.json`, `n*_probe.json` (start-policy rho, H), `mixtures.json`, `rl_setting.json` (S2: Adam lr 3e-4, KL 0.1, 16 prompts x 8 samples, K = 4 tokens). ## Arms and seeds (492 trajectories)
- **A (dense)**: seeds 0, 1, 2; 16 environments x n1 to n4; 400 steps constant; checkpoints every 25 steps (16). n4 is the last stage and optional.
- **pool (ground truth)**: environments e00, e10, m02, m01 (the two highest and two lowest Night 5 final utility at n3, chosen before any run) x n1 to n3; seeds 1000 to 1011; 400 steps constant. Truth = mean of the 12 runs.
- **B (independent)**: same 12 cells; per cell 4 runs of 100 steps (seeds 2000 to 2003), 2 of 200 (2004, 2005), 1 of 400 (2006); constant rate. 1,200 steps per cell, equal to A's 3 x 400.
- **cosL / cosS (schedule control)**: pool environments x n2, n3. cosL: 3 runs, T = 400, seeds 3000 to 3002. cosS: 4 runs T = 100 (3100 to 3103) and 2 runs T = 200 (3200, 3201). lr(u) = lr0 x 0.5 x (1 + cos(pi (u - 1) / T)) for update u = 1..T, floor 0, no warmup. Constant arms for P5: long = A, fresh short = B.
- Utility U = joint exact match of the greedy policy on the 4,000 target prompts minus the start policy's, as a fraction. Budget C = steps x 512 tokens. Per-step logs (prompt index, rewards, token counts, admitted flag, leave-one-out advantage, lr) go to `logs/*.npz` (not committed); A checkpoints at n1 to n3 to `ckpt/` (not committed; n4 not kept).
- Run order (each stage is scorable alone): main n1 to n3, pool n1 to n2, B n1 to n2, cosine n2, pool and B n3, cosine n3, main n4. Driver: `night6.py drive`.

## Fits (identical for prefix and full fits)
- **Eq. 7 law**: `rl4cv.utility_model` design (OLS, standardizers over distinct (scale, env) cells, reference scales (n1, n3) parameter counts), with C0 chosen from a 41-point log grid over 10 to 4,000 steps (x 512 tokens) by training RMSE. `analyze6.FastLaw` is the vectorized copy (tested against `fit_utility_model`).
- **Per-cell saturating form** U = a C / (C + C0), same C0 grid, closed-form a. **Per-cell sigmoid** U = A / (1 + exp(-(log C - log Cmid) / B)), A in [-0.2, 0.3], log Cmid in log(10 to 4,000 steps x 512), B in [0.1, 5], least squares.
- Prefix 50% = checkpoints at steps 25 to 200 (8 points); prefix 25% = steps 25 to 100 (4 points).

## Predictions and scoring (percentage points are fractions x 100)
**P1 (dense versus independent).** Scored on the 12 pool cells (4 environments x n1 to n3). A's estimate = 3-seed mean at each of the 16 checkpoints. B's estimate at budgets 100 / 200 / 400 steps = mean of the final evaluations of its runs of that length; at the other 13 budgets = its 2-parameter saturating fit through those three points. (i) curve RMSE over 12 cells x 16 budgets against the pool mean. (ii) Eq. 7 forecast: fit on the same four environments at n1, n2 (A's or B's estimates at 16 budgets), forecast n3 at 16 budgets, RMSE against the n3 pool mean (4 x 16). Holds if RMSE_A <= RMSE_B + delta for both, delta = one seed SD = mean over the scored cells and budgets of the standard deviation (ddof 1) of the 12 pool runs. Intervals: 2,000 draws, resampling runs within each (arm, scale, env, schedule, length) group and the 4,000 target prompts (one weight draw shared by all runs); the truth's own sampling noise is inside the interval. Reported with n_cells = 12 (4 for (ii)), n_trajectories per arm, 4,000 prompts.

**P2 (prefix extrapolation).** On A, all 16 environments. Primary arm = the Eq. 7 law fitted on n1 to n3 using the 50% prefix versus using all 16 checkpoints. Criterion 1: RMSE over the 48 (scale, env) cells at C_max (400 steps) of the prefix fit <= 1.25 x the full fit's RMSE (full fit is in-sample at C_max). Criterion 2: pairwise rank accuracy (utility_model `pairwise_rank_accuracy`, ties half credit) of the law at the held-out scale at C_max: n4 (fits on n1 to n3) if n4 ran, otherwise n3 (fits on n1, n2), labeled; |RA(prefix) - RA(full)| <= 0.10. The 70% rank threshold is a 02B criterion, not used. P2 holds iff both criteria hold. Secondary, point estimates only: the 25% prefix; per-cell sigmoid and saturating forms scored the same way (RMSE ratio at C_max), by scale and separately for cells with U < 0 at C_max (n4 forgetting cells are reported, never dropped). Interval: 2,000 draws, one seed multiset per scale shared across environments (Night 4 style) and the prompt weights.

**P3 (rolling rho, H).** On A runs at n1 to n3 (144 trajectories). Row = increment U(ck i) to U(ck i+1) for i = 1..14, with features rho_i, H_i from `measure_environment` over the 400 groups (25 steps x 16 prompts) the run generated before checkpoint i. Rows with rho_i = 0 are excluded from every model and counted. Rolling model: dU = c0 + cN x_N + ds (cC + c_rho z(log rho_i) + c_H z(H_i)), ds = s(C_{i+1}) - s(C_i), s = C / (C + C0), C0 from the grid by training SSE, OLS, z means and SDs over training (scale, env, i) cell means. Baselines: dU = 0; dU = previous increment (observed); the start-only law's implied increment (Eq. 7 fitted on cell-mean curves with start-policy rho, H, same C0 rule). Cross-validation: leave-one-environment-out (16 folds) and leave-one-trajectory-out (144 folds; the start-only law is refit without the held-out run). Error = per-trajectory mean squared increment error; paired difference (baseline - rolling) with a 95% bootstrap interval over trajectories (2,000 draws). Holds iff the interval's lower end is above 0 against all three baselines in both CVs. Else NO ADVANTAGE.

**P5 (schedule control).** For b in {100, 200} steps, per cell: effect = mean over long runs of U(b) minus mean over fresh runs of U(b), for constant (A versus B) and cosine (cosL versus cosS); pooled over the 8 cells (4 environments x n2, n3). Statistic: effect_cosine - effect_constant. Clarification made before any outcome (the plan wrote "> 0", but a cosine long run read at step b has not been annealed while its fresh short run has, so the sign is expected to be negative): P5 holds iff the 95% interval of the difference (2,000 draws, runs within group and prompt weights) excludes 0 at both budgets with the same sign; the direction is reported. If P5 fails, the toy cannot see the confound and P1 and P2 are weak evidence.

**Variance decomposition.** On pool runs: per run, residual variance about its own saturating fit (sum of squares / 14) = within-run noise; across-seed variance of the fitted curves, averaged over budgets = seed noise; ratio and the seed SD at C_max reported by scale. A beats B only when within-run noise is small relative to seed noise; the ratio is the transferable number for 02B.

**Outcome label (one, per plan §5):** ADOPT needs P1 and P2 here and the real pilot not contradicting (Stage 2, not part of this task). From the toy alone this task reports: P1 and P2 hold -> "toy supports ADOPT"; P1 holds and P2 fails -> "toy supports ADOPT FOR DENSE CHECKPOINTS ONLY"; P1 fails -> "toy shows NO ADVANTAGE"; WRONG DOMAIN needs the real pilot.

## Known limits (disclosed in advance)
K = 4 tokens per response, all admitted, so rho_i is a constant multiple of mean absolute leave-one-out advantage and the admission and length axis of rho is untestable; KL 0.1 saturates U(C), which flatters saturating extrapolation; n4 is a forgetting regime that deepens at 400 steps; the toy tests statistics, not Lean RL; with 3 seeds there are only 10 distinct seed multisets, so intervals are coarse; pool truth is a 12-run mean.
