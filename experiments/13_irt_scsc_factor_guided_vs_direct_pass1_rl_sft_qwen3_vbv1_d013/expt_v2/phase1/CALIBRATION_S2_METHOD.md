**TLDR:** Freeze this executable method before any new anchor scoring or actual-data fit. The three train anchor policies identify fixed marginal item parameters; a separate conditional hurdle model forecasts the independent second full-policy batch. Missing observations retain their declared slots. Failed identification, calibration or forecast admission disables arm C only, while eligible A/B/P continue.

## Chronology and provenance

This operational implementation follows PROTOCOL section 4, PLAN section 5, L1v2 and the published G1_A3 method. It does not replace their objectives, priors, optimizer, numerical integration or gate thresholds. The S2 float16 probability admission has passed; S1's failure remains final. At this method's preparation, initial anchor generation is active, but no new anchor score, actual-data fit, independent batch-2 call or optimizer update has run.

`locks/CALIBRATION_S2_METHOD.json` binds implementation, tests, dependencies, train allowlist/group features and the existing G1 lock. A subsequent published `CALIBRATION_S2_INPUTS` lock must bind all three completed scoring receipts, private cell ledgers and generation locks before `calibration.py --plan …` can read their outcomes. Every one of 3 × 539 × 8 slots must have a receipt. Library versions and G1's complete source/input binding tree are checked before execution. The plan names the published revisions; a local unpushed lock is insufficient.

Independent batch 2 remains unarmed and its generation directory absent through calibration. The input lock must preserve the method's exact absence checks. The driver creates an exclusive attempt record, retains each partial fit, and refuses a second attempt. No fit is retried with a different seed, prior, subset or optimizer. Input/provenance failures before an attempt do not compute statistics.

## Marginal reward calibration

Policies are 4B Base, half supervised fine-tuning and full supervised fine-tuning, in that order. The train panel contains all 539 tasks and eight responses per policy/task. Declaration and proof fractions retain each output's integer numerator and denominator in the frozen beta-binomial objective. The implementation component is Bernoulli. Unmeasured slots have an explicit false observation mask and zero trials; they are never observed failures. Empty and constant item/policy patterns are reported. Empty or disconnected information cannot identify arm C.

Primary estimates use the frozen maximum-a-posteriori objective. Sensitivity fits use the L1 standard-deviation multipliers 0.5 and 2, changing difficulty/log-discrimination prior scales only, consistently with the existing two-parameter logistic implementation. Each fit uses the same five fixed initializations, positive discrimination bounds [0.1, 5], optimizer tolerances and fixed diagnostic grid. All starts must converge with finite gradients and maximum probability disagreement ≤0.02. Before interpreting fits, numerical differentiation and likelihood/location-scale invariance controls must pass; synthetic recovery uses the actual missingness and denominator pattern, seed 1302041, ability correlation ≥0.80 and probability mean absolute error ≤0.08. Synthetic labels do not reuse observed outcomes.

Primary calibration reports ten equally spaced probability bins, predicted and observed response-fraction means, prediction-minus-outcome bias, absolute error, and denominator-weighted means. Confidence intervals resample complete train task groups, paired across all policies and response slots, for 10,000 draws with seed 1302031. They condition on the fitted predictions and do not include fit uncertainty. The L1 calibration criterion is that the pooled 95% mean-bias interval contains zero. This is an in-sample posterior-point diagnostic, not held-out generalization evidence or proof of exact calibration. Prior-sensitive item changes are reported; the 0.1 descriptive flag does not add an admission threshold. Marginal components are not multiplied into a strict success probability.

The fixed full-policy item weights use `a*(1-q)` normalized across the three marginal components. The existing reward implementation supplies guards, strict-success reward 1 and the weighted geometric mean. Weights remain constant within each eight-response task group. The fraction of constant-strict groups with nonzero weighted-reward variance must be at least 0.15. Unknown eligibility/variance is retained in minimum/maximum bounds; undefined or threshold-straddling bounds cannot admit C.

## Separate strict forecast and F2

The strict model is the fixed conditional conjunction M3. Its extraction/example/nonvacuity, conditional compilation and conditional kernel-honesty risk sets are reconstructed from the frozen scorer fields. Their conjunction must agree with the independent strict oracle on observed outputs. Each risk pattern receives the published synthetic recovery and numerical controls. The primary and two sensitivity fits use the same five initializations and calibration criterion as above. Empty item risk sets are listed and use the unchanged 31-node fixed-prior integration; priors cannot make a failed identification control pass. M2 strict logistic estimates are diagnostic and never replace a failed M3 forecast.

The full-policy anchor ability predicts each task's strict probability `p`; the independent eight-response mixed-group probability is `1-p^8-(1-p)^8`. Before batch 2, the driver writes immutable item parameters, predictions, empirical placebo distribution and F2 through `rl4cv.forecast.freeze_forecast`. F2 remains the preregistered point forecast of area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC) 0.75; the H2 admission threshold remains 0.70. These are different quantities. The placebo distribution uses the exact frozen B reward on measured full1 outputs, with measured/declared counts and every missing slot retained privately, even when C calibration fails. No substitute distribution is fit to batch 2.

The result and artifact hashes must be published before the existing full2 dispatcher can start. A later `H2_S2_INPUTS` lock binds that calibration, its exact predictions/F2, all 539 train tasks/groups and the complete independent scoring receipt. `h2.py --plan …` verifies publication and ancestry, creates one exclusive attempt and never fits anything. A group is known mixed if any measured successes and failures coexist; it is known constant only if all eight responses are measured and equal. All other labels remain unknown.

H2 reports exact point AUC extrema over every binary completion using the already-published A3 association algorithm. Paired task-group bootstrap endpoint bounds use 10,000 draws, seed 1302031; the repeated-copy relaxation is explicitly conservative when labels are missing. Predicted/observed means, bias and mean absolute error have corresponding full-denominator bounds. PASS requires an admitted forecast, no feasible undefined AUC and minimum AUC ≥0.70. Maximum AUC <0.70 is FAIL; other cases are INCONCLUSIVE. C requires PASS. No hypothesis test is added (`p-val=n/a`). G1's independently frozen historical held-out evaluation still runs with its unchanged lock and bounds; neither H2 nor marginal calibration replaces it.

## Execution limits

These are central-processor analyses with zero model calls or device claims. Every new scored input remains private; public records contain counts, method/artifact hashes, aggregate diagnostics and verdicts. Test-source content stays sealed. Completed or interrupted gate attempts are never silently replayed; partial receipts remain available for an honest transition report.
