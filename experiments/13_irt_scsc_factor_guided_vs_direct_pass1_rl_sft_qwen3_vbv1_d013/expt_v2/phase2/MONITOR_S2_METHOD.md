These requirements distinguish an unavailable measurement route from a task whose hidden tests cannot be applied. The earlier helper was preparation, never a pilot execution lock. Bind arm, seed, source, generation randomness, current pre-generation C weights and every scorer/judge receipt. Compare each coverage metric and reward on the exact same paired observed tasks. An observed drop exceeding 10% with increased reward stops the arm. Exactly 10% does not; compare the recorded decimal sums exactly. A zero baseline has no defined relative drop. Do not change rewards or choose another cohort.

Continuation requires complete reward and theorem-count measurements. For each independent coverage route, require its frozen control PASS, terminal baseline and current accounting for all 32 cells, verified cleanup, and a retained receipt for every measured, known compilation-failure, not-applicable, incomplete or unresolved cell. An unrun route or unresolved cleanup cannot establish continuation. Each route must have at least one paired observed coverage value. Known compilation failure has compiled coverage zero; missing or inapplicable coverage is never imputed.

Report all 32 identities privately and counts publicly, the paired observed denominator for every comparison, both paired means and rewards, and full-manifest mean bounds using missing coverage in [0,1]. Theorem-count upper bounds remain unknown if its measurements are missing. A prescribed observed stop remains a stop even if another route is incomplete. Otherwise an unavailable route or insufficient measurement accounting is INCOMPLETE, not evidence of safe continuation.
