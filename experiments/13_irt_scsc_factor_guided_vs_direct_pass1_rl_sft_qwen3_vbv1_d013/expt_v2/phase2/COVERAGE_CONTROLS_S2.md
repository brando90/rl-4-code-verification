**TLDR:** Freeze 50 train-only route-control ratings before any judge call: five tasks, five control types and two unchanged validated judge members. This checks the hosted transport and judge behavior; it is not an experiment efficacy result or permission to skip the independent train mutation/coverage prerequisites.

Its external VeriBench checkout is the project-pinned `c11b99badfa386fd26810b208eef1eb8dc9f6806` in this writer's own clone. No benchmark reference is edited.

The selector is the existing first eligible train task per core family, in the frozen allowlist order, with at least two gold theorem statements. The five variants are oracle, vacuous, shuffled, half-deleted and blank. Each member rates each control once, as the existing route-control implementation specifies: 25 ratings per member, 50 total. Actual formal specification coverage retains its validated three identical repeats per member, median within member, then mean of both medians divided by five. Route controls do not replace that aggregation.

The members remain GPT-6 Sol at high reasoning effort and Claude Opus 5.5 at maximum compute effort, with the unchanged no-fallback selector, maximum output tokens, human-mirror rubric and task-excluding demonstrations. Both model selectors were present without configured fallbacks in the authenticated [metadata-only check](coverage_registry_metadata.json). Its HTTP 200 read made zero model calls and does not imply model-query or scoring success.

Each extreme control requires at least 90% agreement with its frozen rule: oracle score at least 4/5, vacuous and shuffled at most 1/5, blank exactly 0/5. Half-deleted requires at least 80% in the inclusive 1–4/5 range. With five tasks, these require 5/5 for each extreme and 4/5 for half-deleted, separately for each member. Every declared receipt and exact request envelope must be measured and verified. These are fixed technical-control counts, not an efficacy hypothesis test; p-val=n/a.
