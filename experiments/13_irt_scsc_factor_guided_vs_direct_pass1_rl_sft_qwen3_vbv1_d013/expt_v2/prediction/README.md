# Version 2 prediction implementation before L1v2

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/rl-4-code-verification/blob/main/experiments/13_irt_scsc_factor_guided_vs_direct_pass1_rl_sft_qwen3_vbv1_d013/expt_v2/prediction/README.md>

**TLDR:** Implement the protocol's five forecast models, grouped inner selection, conditional risk sets, train-anchor marginal models and fixed group weights before measuring historical validation or test factors. All numerical settings are in `config.json` and will be hashed by L1v2.

`irt_v2.py` fits maximum-a-posteriori two-parameter logistic item response models with exact mean-zero/unit-variance ability identification and positive bounded discrimination. Analytic likelihood gradients have central-difference tests. New-item predictions integrate difficulty and log-discrimination random effects by 31-point normal-quantile quadrature, conditional on training-fitted hierarchy coefficients; this empirical-Bayes approximation does not integrate uncertainty in those fitted coefficients. Every fit uses the same five fixed starts. Prior standard-deviation multipliers 0.5 and 2 are reported as sensitivity analyses, never selected from forecast accuracy.

`forecast_v2.py` supplies M0's Beta(1,1) baseline, M1's training-only monotone calibration, M2's strict model, and M3/M3e's three conditional hurdles. Declaration/proof fractions are not multiplied into the strict likelihood. Empty and constant risk sets are explicit, unsupported new items use integrated random effects, and a disagreement between the stated hurdle chain and the historical strict oracle blocks fitted-condition admission without changing that oracle. This check matters because the already-authored base-vacuity fixture establishes source-specific oracle semantics that must not be silently redefined by components.

`nested_forecast_v2.py` compares the same full-schedule cells under five task-group folds and full-schedule policy holdouts. Every fit and inner selection excludes all full-schedule outcomes. Three inner group folds select M3 versus M3e on the last earlier rung (75%), fitted using lower rungs. Full-schedule predictions use the latest available earlier rung (75%); G0 independently retains its specified 50% → 100% comparison. For a group-held-out item, M0 uses the training-fold pooled mean as the protocol specifies. M1 receives earlier-rung reward as a covariate. Held-out item effects are never fitted from target outcomes. Test rows are rejected before constructing the prediction table.

`evaluation_v2.py` preserves G1's two paired log-loss requirements and policy-Pearson threshold of 0.80 for both holdout schemes. It reports Pearson, Spearman, Kendall, absolute error, mean bias, observed/predicted means, calibration bins and task-group intervals with 10,000 paired draws. Missing candidate predictions fail admission while available baselines and M2 remain reported. Intervals condition on the fitted forecasts and do not resample model-fitting uncertainty. Correlation over three available full-schedule sizes is descriptive and unstable.

Two previously unspecified implementation controls are made explicit prospectively, before L1v2 or historical validation factors: the mean-bias calibration diagnostic must have a task-group 95% interval containing zero, and synthetic recovery on the actual later risk-count/missingness pattern must have ability correlation at least 0.80 and mean probability error at most 0.08. These are owner-specified operational controls for the protocol's calibration/recovery prerequisites, not changes to its G0/G1/H2 effect thresholds. Passing a zero-containing interval is not proof of exact calibration. Recovery uses one fixed synthetic replicate and cannot establish identification from priors alone.

`marginal_v2.py` separately fits the train anchor components: per-output beta-binomial command/proof counts and Bernoulli implementation success. Its five-start checks, train-only guard, positive normalized weights, constant-weight equivalence, replay and earlier-update-only ability refit have deterministic controls. Weights are fixed per group; the caller refits every ten updates from preceding train rollouts with item parameters frozen. Any unsupported or failed fit disables C under the protocol, without substituting weights.

Validation so far uses authored synthetic arrays only. Actual-risk-pattern recovery, prior sensitivity, posterior-predictive calibration and G1 are still unmeasured; they run in their admitted phase. The code does not authorize skipping LP-1 or the required anchor/calibration/F2/batch-2 ordering.
