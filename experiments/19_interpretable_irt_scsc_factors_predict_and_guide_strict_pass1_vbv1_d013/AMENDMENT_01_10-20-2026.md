## 5. Disclosure and sensitivity: the token cap in G1

The pinned start-policy file's `k_capped` column (samples that stopped at the 12,288-token cap) shows:

- 52 of 135 validation tasks have at least 1 of 8 samples capped;
- 5 tasks are capped on all 8;
- 13 tasks have at least 4 of 8 capped, and all 13 have zero strict successes.

Capping, rather than task difficulty as other systems see it, may explain some of the start policy's failures. That would hurt any difficulty-based forecast. A cap, though, is a property the RL prompt pool would inherit.

**Pre-registered descriptive sensitivity:**

- **What it computes:** G1's primary AUC, the two heuristic AUCs and the two IRT-specific contrasts, recomputed without the 13 tasks that have ≥ 4 of 8 capped samples. That leaves 122 tasks: 32 mixed and 90 not mixed.
- **Statistics:** 10,000 bootstrap draws over the remaining validation groups, seed 20261021.
- **Code:** `expt_v1/sensitivity_cap_e19.py`, committed with this amendment before any output is read. It reads only the run's per-task G1 table and the pinned start-policy file.
- **Scope:** descriptive. It does not change the G1 verdict.
